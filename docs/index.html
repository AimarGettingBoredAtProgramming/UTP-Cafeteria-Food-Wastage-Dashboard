      Live data rules

      • Weather: the Open-Meteo hourly forecast for UTP (4.385° N, 100.979° E), up to 16 days ahead. Meal windows are lunch 11:00–15:00 and dinner 17:00–21:00.
      • Heavy rain: at least 2.5 mm of rain in the meal window, or a rain chance of at least 75% with 1 mm or more.
      • Haze: average PM2.5 of at least 35 µg/m³ in the meal window (Malaysian 24-hour standard), from the Open-Meteo air-quality forecast (about 5 days ahead).
      • Calendar: exam weeks, study week, breaks and Perak public holidays come from docs/data/academic_calendar.js.
      • Beyond the forecast range, or if you're offline, the weather falls back to Clear. You can override any live value by hand.

      Assumptions (editable)

      • Likert answers are scored 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1 (very unlikely → very likely).
      • The normal-weekday baseline is days eaten at the cafeteria per week (capped at 5) ÷ 5 = 0.72.
      • Factors are multiplied together, and demand never goes below 20%.
      • Risk tiers: Low ≥ 80% · Medium ≥ 55% · High below.

      Limitations & ethics

      • Small, skewed sample: 56 students, mostly Year 2 and Village 5 residents.
      • Stated, not observed, behaviour: students reported what they would do.
      • No sales data: the training data is simulated from survey weights.
      • Independence assumption: combined conditions may be over- or under-estimated.
      • Forecasts can be wrong: rain timing in particular is uncertain, so treat the advice as a guide.
      • Ethics: the survey was anonymous and voluntary, with no identifiable data. All outputs are estimates, and the vendor decides.
      • Next step: log real cooked, sold and leftover counts with 1–2 vendors for 2–4 weeks, then retrain on that data.
