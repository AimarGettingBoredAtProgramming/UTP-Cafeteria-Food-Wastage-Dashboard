      Assumptions (editable)

      Illustrative values for the energy and cost estimates. Replace them with measured vendor figures when available.

      • Likert answers are scored 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1 (very unlikely → very likely).
      • Normal-weekday baseline = days per week eaten at the cafeteria (capped at 5) ÷ 5 = 0.72.
      • Factors are treated as independent and multiplied; demand is floored at 20%.
      • Risk tiers: Low ≥ 80% · Medium ≥ 55% · High below.

      Limitations & ethics

      • Small, skewed sample: 56 students, mostly Year 2 and Village 5 residents.
      • Stated, not observed behaviour: students reported what they would do.
      • No sales data: training data is simulated from survey weights, so the tree learns survey-derived rules.
      • Independence assumption: combined conditions (e.g. rainy exam Friday) may be over- or under-estimated.
      • Cannot predict sudden events such as campus programmes or outages.
      • Ethics: anonymous, voluntary survey; no names or identifiable data; outputs labelled as estimates; the vendor makes the final call.
      • Next step: log real cooked / sold / leftover numbers with 1–2 vendors for 2–4 weeks and retrain.
